# Classic Jenga, as printed in the box.

Build the tower: 18 layers of 3 blocks, alternate layers crosswise. Take turns.
On your turn take one block from any level except the one directly beneath the
highest finished level -- wait: not the highest finished level itself, and nothing
above it. Use one hand only at a time. Put the block on the very top, in the
pattern of the layers: complete a level of three before you begin the next one. A
block may not be pushed back into the middle of the tower. The game is over when
the tower falls.
