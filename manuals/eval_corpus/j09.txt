# Spiral -- 9 pieces, 3 x 3, placed clockwise round the picture.
# Pieces are named by where they belong in the box picture: r<row>c<col>, counting
# from r0c0 in the top-left. Each piece goes in its own cell of the board.

Begin at the top-left piece and go clockwise round the edge, each piece after the
previous: r0c0, r0c1, r0c2, r1c2, r2c2, r2c1, r2c0, r1c0. The centre piece r1c1
closes the spiral.
