# Market -- 20 pieces, 5 across, 4 down.
# Pieces are named by where they belong in the box picture: r<row>c<col>, counting
# from r0c0 in the top-left. Each piece goes in its own cell of the board.

Work from the outside in. Place the four corners first. Then do the rest of the
edge pieces in any order. Only then fill the six interior pieces (rows 2 and 3,
columns 2 to 4 -- that is r1c1, r1c2, r1c3, r2c1, r2c2, r2c3).
