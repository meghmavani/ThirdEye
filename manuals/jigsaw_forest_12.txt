# Forest -- 12-piece jigsaw, 4 pieces across and 3 down.
# Pieces are named by where they belong in the box picture: r<row>c<col>, counting
# from the top-left piece r0c0. The board has a cell for every piece.

Work from the outside in.

1. Place the four corner pieces first: r0c0 in the top-left corner of the board, r0c3
   in the top-right, r2c0 in the bottom-left and r2c3 in the bottom-right.
2. Once all four corners are down, fill in the rest of the border. Along the top edge
   place r0c1 then r0c2. Along the bottom edge place r2c1 then r2c2. Down the left
   edge place r1c0, and down the right edge place r1c3.
3. Only when the whole border is complete, fill the middle with the last two pieces:
   r1c1, then r1c2.
