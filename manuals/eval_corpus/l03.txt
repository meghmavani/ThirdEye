# Step pyramid
# 8x8 baseplate. Coordinates: studs from the bottom-left corner; a
# brick's position is its lowest-x, lowest-y stud.

1. Place a white 2x4 brick on the plate in the bottom-left corner, long side away from you.
2. Place a second white 2x4 brick directly to its right.
3. Bridge them with a black 2x4 turned sideways so it runs left to right across the front of both, on top.
4. Put a green 2x2 brick on top of the left white brick, behind the black one.
