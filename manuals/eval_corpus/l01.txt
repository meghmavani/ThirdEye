# Three in a row
# 8x8 baseplate. Coordinates: studs from the bottom-left corner; a
# brick's position is its lowest-x, lowest-y stud.

1. Place a red 2x4 brick on the plate in the bottom-left corner, long side pointing away from you.
2. Place a blue 2x4 brick beside it, two studs to the right, the same way round.
3. Place a green 2x4 brick beside that, two studs further right.
