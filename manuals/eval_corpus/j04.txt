# Harbour -- 12 pieces, 4 columns by 3 rows.
# Legend: the top row is r0c0 r0c1 r0c2 r0c3, the middle row r1c0 .. r1c3, the
# bottom row r2c0 .. r2c3.

Start with the four corner pieces: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.
After that, complete the rest of the frame: the two pieces between the top
corners, the two between the bottom corners, and the single piece on each side
(middle row, first and last column). Do not start the picture's middle until the
frame is complete. The last two pieces are the middle of the middle row, left
one first.
