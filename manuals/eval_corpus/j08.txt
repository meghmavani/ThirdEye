# Kite -- 9 pieces, 3 x 3, one piece goes in turned.
# Pieces are named by where they belong in the box picture: r<row>c<col>, counting
# from r0c0 in the top-left. Each piece goes in its own cell of the board.

1. The four corners first (r0c0, r0c2, r2c0, r2c2).
2. The four edge pieces (r0c1, r1c0, r1c2, r2c1).
3. The centre piece r1c1 last. It is the only one that is not upright: turn it a
   quarter turn clockwise (90 degrees) before you set it down.
